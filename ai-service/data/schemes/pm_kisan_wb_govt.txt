Scheme - Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) provides income support of Rs 6,000 per year to eligible farmer families, paid in three equal instalments. It aims to supplement farmers' financial needs for agricultural inputs and household expenses, thereby improving their livelihood and productivity.

Benefits Offer Under the Scheme

Provides direct income support of Rs 6,000 per year to eligible farming families, paid as Direct Benefits Transfer (DBT) in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each.
Eligibility

Must be a citizen of India belonging to a farmer family and owning cultivable land.
Ownership of land should be recorded in the land records of the concerned State/UT.
Excluded categories include institutional landholders and higher-income groups such as serving or retired government officials (except certain classes), income tax payers, and professionals like doctors, engineers, and lawyers.
The beneficiary must have a valid Aadhaar number and an active bank account for receiving Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
